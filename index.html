Gymnastics & Japanese Cultural Etiquette

Phrases and manners to use during training, team meals, and cultural exchange in Niigata!

Encouragement
Ganbatte! 💪

がんばれ / がんばって

"Do your best!" or "Good luck!" (Say this when teammates step onto apparatus).

Polite Greeting
Arigatou Gozaimasu! 🙏

ありがとうございます

"Thank you very much!" (Say this to coaches after receiving feedback or assistance).

Gymnastics
Hai! 🖐️

はい！

"Yes!" (Crisp, polite response when coach calls your name or gives instructions).

Meal Etiquette
Itadakimasu! 🥢

いただきます

"I humbly receive this food!" (Say this before starting every meal with your hands together).

Meal Etiquette
Gochisosama-deshita! 🍵

ごちそうさまでした

"Thank you for the meal!" (Say this after finishing breakfast or dinner).

Manners & Respect
Sumimasen! 🙋‍♀️

すみません

"Excuse me / Sorry" (Use when passing someone, getting attention, or asking for help).

Compliments
Sugoi & Kawaii! ✨

すごい / かわいい

"Amazing!" & "Cute!" (Praise teammates for great skills or cute stationery).

Teamwork
Otsukaresama-desu! 👏

おつかれさまでした

"Great work today!" (Say this to team members and coaches at the end of training).

Daily Greetings
Ohayou Gozaimasu! ☀️

おはようございます

"Good morning!" (Greeting coaches, teammates, and hotel staff every morning).
