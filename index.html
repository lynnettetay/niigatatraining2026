Guidelines for Athletes & Parents

Gymnastics Training Exchange in Nagaoka City & Joetsu City, Niigata Prefecture, Japan. Please review and acknowledge these guidelines to ensure a safe, enjoyable, and well-organised trip.

Emergency & Contacts Reference

Keep Head Coach and accompanying staff emergency details accessible at all times.

Jump to Acknowledgment

1 Team Discipline

This overseas training exchange is organised by Stardust Gymnastics Club (Singapore). Throughout the trip, all athletes are expected to follow the instructions and arrangements of the Head Coach and accompanying staff. Training and activity schedules may be adjusted depending on weather conditions, venue arrangements, transportation, or other circumstances.

Athletes are expected to:

  • Be at least 10 minutes early for all assemblies, training sessions, meals, and group activities.
  • Remain with the team at all times, unless permission has been given by a coach.
  • Inform a coach before leaving any designated area.
  • Cooperate with any necessary changes to the programme.

2 Safety Priority

The safety of our athletes is our priority. During outings and activities, athletes must remain with the group and follow the coaches' instructions.

If permission is given to leave the group:

  • Travel in groups of at least two.
  • Inform the coach of destination & return time.

Emergencies & Illness:

Any illness, injury, lost belongings, or emergency must be reported to the Head Coach immediately.

* Athletes must follow Japanese laws and local regulations, observe traffic rules, and behave responsibly in public areas. Keep hotel info accessible at all times.

3 Training Expectations

Approach every training session with a positive attitude and make the most of this opportunity in Nagaoka and Joetsu.

  • Follow daily training programme and coaches' instructions.
  • Respect both Singaporean and Japanese coaches.
  • Complete proper warm-ups and cool-downs.
  • Inform a coach immediately if experiencing pain, discomfort, or injury.
  • Take care of all training equipment and facilities.
  • Respect Japanese athletes, coaches, and other sharing clubs with good sportsmanship.

6 Etiquette & Conduct

As guests in Japan, we ask everyone to respect Japanese culture, customs, and public etiquette.

  • Be polite and greet coaches, staff, and other athletes respectfully.
  • Keep public areas clean and dispose of rubbish appropriately.
  • Avoid disruptive behaviour in hotels, public transport, and venues.
  • Privacy Rule: Do not photograph, record, or post other athletes, coaches, or training sessions without explicit permission.

7 Parent Cooperation

  • Ensure emergency contacts remain reachable.
  • Inform Head Coach in advance of medical conditions, allergies, or dietary requirements.
  • Go through guidelines with child prior to departure.

8 Important Notes

Program changes may occur due to weather, transport, or safety. Repeated failure to follow team safety instructions will result in coaching intervention and parental contact.

Japanese Words & Cultural Etiquette

Phrases and manners for training with Japanese coaches and teammates!

Gymnastics Gym Words

Ganbatte! (がんばって!) Do your best!
Hai! (はい!) Yes! / Understood!
Arigatou gozaimasu Thank you very much
Yoroshiku onegaishimasu Please coach/help me!

Mealtime & Daily Etiquette

Itadakimasu (いただきます) Said before eating
Gochisosama deshita Thank you for the meal
Sumimasen (すみません) Excuse me / Sorry
Otsukaresama desu Great work today team!

Camp Etiquette Tips 🇯🇵

  • Take off outdoor shoes: Always change into gym slippers or bare feet at entryways (*Genkan*).
  • Bowing: A slight bow when greeting coaches or saying thank you shows great respect.
  • Keep it clean: Take your trash with you and clean up your apparatus after training!
